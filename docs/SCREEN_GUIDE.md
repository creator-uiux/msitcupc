# Screen guide

One entry per product screen. The heading is the source file. Every entry
answers three questions: what the screen is, what it is for, and how to use it.
The screen book reads this file, so the PDF and the explanations stay in step.
The development Gallery at `index.html` is not a product screen and has no
entry.

## login.html

**What it is.** The sign-in page for UPMail accounts, with the four demo accounts below the form.

**What it is for.** Start a session as a real UPMail user or as one of the demo roles.

**How to use it.**

1. Choose Sign in with UPMail and use an `@upcebu.edu.ph` account.
2. Or select a demo account labelled Demo only.
3. If the account has no provisioned role, the page says Access not granted.

## 404.html

**What it is.** The not-found page shown when an address does not exist or has moved.

**What it is for.** Recover from a mistyped or retired route.

**How to use it.**

1. Check the address for a typo.
2. Choose Back to sign in to start again.
3. Or choose Open dashboard when your session is still valid.

## offline.html

**What it is.** The offline fallback shown when an installed copy cannot reach the network.

**What it is for.** Explain the connection state and let you retry without losing submitted work.

**How to use it.**

1. Check the connection.
2. Choose Retry, or wait; the page reloads when the network returns.
3. Use Go to the dashboard once the connection is back.

## or-dashboard.html

**What it is.** Your landing screen with the action queue, eight status links, quick actions, due maintenance and recent history.

**What it is for.** See what needs your office and start the next step without hunting through menus.

**How to use it.**

1. Read Needs your action and open the record that needs a decision.
2. Use a status link to review that slice of the register.
3. Use a quick action for New Request, My Requests, Confirmation or Reports.
4. Check due maintenance and recent updates.
5. The office lane hides the activity chart; the status breakdown fills the row.

## or-request.html

**What it is.** The intake form for a new request, with your recent requests and the reply and withdrawal controls.

**What it is for.** Raise a request for your office and answer a question on an existing ticket.

**How to use it.**

1. Choose a category, describe the issue and optionally pick an asset.
2. Attach evidence when it helps, within the file limits.
3. Submit; the system issues the ticket number and sets Submitted.
4. For a Needs Information record, reply on the same ticket.
5. For an active record, choose Request withdrawal and record the reason.

## or-tickets.html

**What it is.** The register for your lane with search, filters, sortable columns, the column picker, pagination and CSV export.

**What it is for.** Find the requests your office may see and open the full record.

**How to use it.**

1. Search or filter to narrow the list.
2. Sort and choose the columns you need.
3. Open a row to read the record.
4. Export CSV; it mirrors the columns you were shown.

## or-ticket.html

**What it is.** One request in full: the five-step track, status and merged history, conversation, attachments, impact and urgency, diagnosis, costs and testing.

**What it is for.** Read what happened and reply, attach evidence or ask to withdraw.

**How to use it.**

1. Read the track, status and history.
2. Write a reply in the composer; replies reach the IT lanes.
3. Attach evidence if needed.
4. For an active request, choose Request withdrawal and record the reason.
5. Internal notes are never shown to an office session.

## or-confirm.html

**What it is.** The office decision queue for resolved work, availability answers, reminders, recurrence and maintenance visit confirmation.

**What it is for.** Decide whether a fix is accepted and answer availability questions.

**How to use it.**

1. Open a resolved record in the queue.
2. Accept and record the confirming person; the ticket closes.
3. Or reject with feedback; it returns to In Progress.
4. No response keeps it Resolved and the system sends a reminder.
5. For a maintenance visit, confirm availability or agree a new date.

## or-visits.html

**What it is.** The read-only list of maintenance visits planned for your office, with dates, technicians and status.

**What it is for.** See when a technician is coming and which visits are complete.

**How to use it.**

1. Read the visit list and the status of each visit.
2. Check the date and the assigned technician.
3. Use Confirmation and Closure to answer availability or confirm a completed visit.

## or-register.html

**What it is.** The read-only register of corrective tickets and preventive jobs for your office.

**What it is for.** Review the office's full history without changing it.

**How to use it.**

1. Filter the register to the records you need.
2. Open a row to read the record.
3. Export CSV when you need a copy of the list.

## or-reports.html

**What it is.** The office metrics and the eight-report catalogue with filters and CSV export.

**What it is for.** Read volume, service and maintenance measures for your office.

**How to use it.**

1. Choose a report.
2. Set the filters and the period.
3. Read the summary and the rows.
4. Export CSV; the export carries the current scope.

## or-notifications.html

**What it is.** The full notification inbox; the bell is only the two-line preview.

**What it is for.** Read every notice addressed to you and clear the bell.

**How to use it.**

1. Open Notifications from the top bar or the navigation.
2. Read an item and follow its link to the record.
3. Choose Mark all read; this clears the bell for you only.

## or-profile.html

**What it is.** Your contact details, the password panel, and appearance and session preferences.

**What it is for.** Keep your contact information and personal settings current.

**How to use it.**

1. Review the contact fields.
2. Change the password in its panel.
3. Choose the appearance and session preferences.

## or-flow.html

**What it is.** The swimlane diagram of the documented process, with the coverage proof for every step.

**What it is for.** Understand which lane owns the next step; this page is reference only.

**How to use it.**

1. Choose Show all lanes or My lane.
2. Read the lane and the decision branches.
3. Use the coverage proof to trace a step back to the specification.
4. The guide is not in lane navigation; open its URL directly.

## or-roles.html

**What it is.** The four roles, what each may do, and the route permission reference.

**What it is for.** Check a permission or route before asking someone to act.

**How to use it.**

1. Read the role summary.
2. Compare the capability rows.
3. Use the route reference to see which page belongs to which role.
4. The page is reference only and is not in lane navigation.

## itc-dashboard.html

**What it is.** Your workload at a glance: the action queue, workload and status counts, maintenance due and recent activity.

**What it is for.** Start the shift by seeing which assigned jobs need work.

**How to use it.**

1. Read the queue that needs your action.
2. Open a job or ticket.
3. Check the maintenance due list.
4. Use the status counts to find held or active work.

## itc-tickets.html

**What it is.** The register for your records with search, filters, sorting, the column picker and CSV export.

**What it is for.** Find an assigned or completed record and open it.

**How to use it.**

1. Filter or search to your records.
2. Sort and choose the columns you need.
3. Open a row to read the record.
4. Export CSV of the current view.

## itc-ticket.html

**What it is.** One ticket in full, including the conversation, evidence and the internal note composer.

**What it is for.** Work the record and document the repair.

**How to use it.**

1. Read the status and history.
2. Coordinate the schedule with the office.
3. Record the diagnosis, repair, cost and evidence.
4. Post a public reply or an internal note.
5. Record the test; a pass moves the ticket to Resolved.

## itc-technician.html

**What it is.** The workbench for the ticket you are servicing: schedule, diagnosis, repair actions, test result, dependency and next step.

**What it is for.** Move an assigned ticket from coordination through diagnosis and repair to the test.

**How to use it.**

1. Coordinate the schedule with the office.
2. Choose Start work.
3. Record the diagnosis and the repair.
4. Place the work On Hold with the dependency when it is blocked.
5. Record the test result; a pass sets Resolved.

## itc-maintenance.html

**What it is.** Schedules, generated jobs, checklist execution, next due dates and the additional fault hand-off.

**What it is for.** Complete an assigned preventive maintenance visit and record its checklist.

**How to use it.**

1. Open the assigned maintenance job.
2. Record each checklist item with its result and findings.
3. Answer the additional fault question; yes creates a linked ticket.
4. Record the outcome; a complete checklist writes Successful.
5. The office then confirms the visit.

## itc-register.html

**What it is for.** Review history across your assigned offices.

**How to use it.**

3. Export CSV when you need a copy.
4. The page does not change a record.

## ic-dashboard.html

**What it is.** Every office at a glance: the review queue, holds, due maintenance, workload and activity.

**What it is for.** Monitor the whole workflow and find where your decision is needed.

**How to use it.**

1. Open a request in the review queue.
2. Check the on-hold dependencies and their next action.
3. Check due maintenance.
4. Compare offices with the workload cards.
5. Use the activity chart to read volume.

## ic-request.html

**What it is.** The intake form used to raise a request on behalf of an office.

**What it is for.** Record a request that arrived by phone, email or walk-in.

**How to use it.**

1. Choose the office, requester and source.
2. Choose a category and describe the issue.
3. Optionally pick an asset and attach evidence.
4. Submit; the ticket enters Submitted and appears in Review and Assign.

## ic-tickets.html

**What it is.** Every record with filters, sorting, the column picker, bulk assign and CSV export.

**What it is for.** Find, compare and reassign records across all offices.

**How to use it.**

1. Search or filter the records.
2. Select rows for bulk assign.
3. Open a record for the full view.
4. Export CSV with the current scope.

## ic-ticket.html

**What it is.** One ticket in full with the conversation, evidence and the next step.

**What it is for.** Act as coordinator on a record: request information, assign, follow up, cancel or close.

**How to use it.**

1. Read the track and history.
2. Request information when the request is incomplete.
3. Assess and assign a technician.
4. Follow up or resolve a dependency.
5. Record cancellation or authorized closure when the workflow allows.

## ic-review.html

**What it is.** The coordinator workbench for a Submitted request.

**What it is for.** Decide whether the request is complete and who should do the work.

**How to use it.**

1. Pick a Submitted request.
2. Choose Request information if it is incomplete.
3. Record impact, urgency, priority and the target date.
4. Assign a technician.
5. Record sign-off when the category needs approval.

## ic-maintenance.html

**What it is.** Schedules, generated jobs, pause and resume, and the next due date.

**What it is for.** Plan preventive maintenance and keep the cadence correct.

**How to use it.**

1. Create a schedule with the office, assets, checklist, frequency, due date and technician.
2. Update the plan when the cadence or technician changes.
3. Pause or resume the plan.
4. Run the schedule check by hand; it never duplicates an occurrence.
5. Close a visit with a reason when the coordinator must.

## ic-confirm.html

**What it is.** The closure queue with reminders, authorized closure, recurrence and maintenance visit closure.

**What it is for.** Keep resolved work moving when the office has not answered.

**How to use it.**

1. Open a resolved record.
2. Send a reminder; the status stays Resolved.
3. Use authorized closure with a reason when policy allows.
4. Create a linked follow-up from a Closed record.
5. Close a maintenance visit with a reason when required.

## ic-register.html

**What it is.** The central register of every ticket and job across all offices.

**What it is for.** Read the whole record set without changing it.

**How to use it.**

1. Filter the register.
2. Open a record to read its history.
3. Export CSV with the current scope.

## mgmt-users.html

**What it is.** The accounts register: person, role, office and active state.

**What it is for.** Provision people and keep their access correct.

**How to use it.**

1. Search for a person.
2. Create or edit an account.
3. Set the role, office and active state.
4. Deactivate an account instead of deleting it.
5. Renaming a person cascades to records that name them.

## mgmt-offices.html

**What it is.** Office records, codes, contacts and active state.

**What it is for.** Maintain the offices that requests, assets and reports refer to.

**How to use it.**

1. Find or create an office.
2. Set its code and contacts.
3. Set the office's active state.
4. Renaming an office cascades to records and assets.

## mgmt-assets.html

**What it is.** The unit register: number, name, category, holding office and location.

**What it is for.** Keep the asset list that intake and maintenance refer to.

**How to use it.**

1. Register a unit with its number, name, category, office and location.
2. Use the AA-AA-00 form for the number; it can be registered once.
3. Retire a unit instead of deleting it so old tickets still resolve.
4. Intake offers registered numbers as suggestions.

## mgmt-settings.html

**What it is.** Settings and reference data: reminders, working week, closed dates, service level, approval categories, priority matrix, closure roles, categories, sources, priorities and frequencies.

**What it is for.** Configure the rules the workflow reads without changing the permission map.

**How to use it.**

1. Adjust a setting and save it.
2. Add or edit the reference rows.
3. Remember that configured role actions may narrow the map but never widen it.
4. Settings apply to future actions; the audit trail keeps past events.

## mgmt-audit.html

**What it is.** The append-only history of who did what and when.

**What it is for.** Investigate a change or export the trail.

**How to use it.**

1. Filter by actor, subject or event.
2. Read the event detail.
3. Export the filtered list to CSV.
4. No client can edit or delete an event.
